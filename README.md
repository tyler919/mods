# Tyler's Minecraft mods: bugs, ideas and feedback

This is the place to report a bug, suggest an idea, or tell me something feels unbalanced in any of my mods.
There's no code here, just your posts.

**[Open a new issue →](https://github.com/tyler919/mods/issues/new/choose)**

You'll pick one of three forms:

| Form | Use it when |
|---|---|
| 🐞 **Bug report** | Something crashes, breaks, or doesn't do what it should |
| 💡 **Idea / feature request** | You want something added or changed |
| ⚖️ **Balance problem** | Something is too strong, too weak, too expensive or too slow |

Every form asks which mod it's about, so your post lands in the right pile.

## Mods covered

- **Colossal Furnace**: a giant furnace add-on for Jumbo Furnace
- **Colossal Quarry**: a multiblock quarry rig
- **Colossal Apiary**: a late-game Productive Bees add-on
- **XP Works**: liquid XP, tanks and drains
- **Radio Works**: radios and speakers
- **MVD 2**: the modpack

## Before you post

- **Search first.** If someone already posted your idea, give it a 👍 instead of posting it again. I look at the 👍 counts when I pick what goes in the next update.
- **For crashes,** upload your crash report or `latest.log` to [mclo.gs](https://mclo.gs) and paste the link.
- **One thing per post** makes it much easier to track and close when it's fixed.

## What happens next

I read everything. If I decide to build it, the issue gets the **planned** label. When an update with it comes out, I close the
issue with the version it's in, and GitHub notifies you.
