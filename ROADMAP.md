# DecayPlay's mods: roadmap

What's out, what's next and what's planned for each of my Minecraft mods. There are no dates: an update comes out
when it's built and tested.

Want something on this list? **[Post an idea](https://github.com/tyler919/mods/issues/new/choose)**, or give an
existing one a 👍. I look at the 👍 counts when I pick what goes in the next update.

| Word | Means |
|---|---|
| **Out now** | On CurseForge |
| **Being built** | Being made right now, for the next update |
| **Planned** | Decided, coming in a later update |
| **Ideas** | Being considered, not promised yet |

**Prefer clicking around?** The same roadmap is an [interactive board](https://github.com/users/tyler919/projects/2), with a tab for each mod.

## Colossal Quarry

**Out now: 2.2.0** (NeoForge, 1.21.1; the Forge file stays on 1.2.0; needs DecayPlay's Guidebooks). New in 2.2.0: an
in-game guidebook, the Colossal Quarry Handbook. You get it the first time you join a world. 36 pages in 6 chapters:
Start here takes you from the first casing to the first hole one step at a time, and every build and machine has a
guided page with what you need, numbered steps with a 3D view of the build, "Done when" and "If it doesn't work". Show
in world sets a see-through ghost of the build down in your world to build into: right blocks vanish, wrong ones glow
red, and a list says what's still missing. Hold G over any Colossal Quarry item to open its page. It replaces the
hold-W guides (Ponder), so Ponder and Flywheel are no longer inside the mod and the file is smaller. Worlds and
configs from 2.1, 2.0 and 1.x carry over.

**Planned: 3.0, the full rig**
- Bigger rigs: the Heavy Rig (7×7×7) and the Colossal Rig (9×9×9)
- Drill Heads that temper as they dig and grow into the next head
- Overdrive, kept cool with water
- Survey Scanner, and a Survey button that predicts the whole dig
- Dense Silo Casing (4× the space)
- Advancements and a full set of sounds
- A charge slot on the generators, and optional CC:Tweaked support

**Ideas from players**

## Colossal Furnace

**Out now: 1.5.0** (NeoForge 1.21.1; needs Jumbo Furnace and DecayPlay's Guidebooks). New in 1.5.0: an in-game
guidebook, the Colossal Furnace Guide. You get it the first time you join a world. It takes you through the whole mod
one step at a time: the Jumbo Furnace, the Fragment Mold, Fragments, Colossal Furnace Blocks, the 6×6×6 build,
hoppers, lava from tanks and pipes, the Liquid Experience tank, every tier and the Heart, up to Tier 20. Show in world
puts a see-through copy of the furnace on the ground to build into: a wrong block glows red, and it tells you when the
build is complete. Hover any Colossal Furnace item and hold G to open its page. The numbers in the book are your
world's numbers. It replaces the hold-W guides (Ponder), so Ponder and Flywheel are no longer inside the jar. Worlds
and configs from 1.4.1 load unchanged.

## Radio Works

## Atomic Generation

**Out now: 0.1.0-m8** (NeoForge 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.200 or newer, on both client and server; a beta build on
CurseForge): milestones 1 to 8 of Atomic Generation 1.0, "First Criticality". Needs DecayPlay's Guidebooks 1.0.0 or
newer; JEI, Jade, WTHIT and Curios are optional. Everything 0.1.0-m7 had (the real nuclear fuel cycle from uranium ore
to fuel, radiation in sieverts with shielding and contamination, the steam power side, the Chicago Pile, the research
tree, Magnox with its fuel pool, dry cask and fuel transfer crane, the research pool reactor and its experiments, the
RBMK with its real traps, the Reactor Operator's Handbook and Operator's Goggles) plus **the accidents**: each reactor
now fails the way its real design failed. Played carefully, you never meet them. Damage shows step by step (Normal,
Alarm, Fuel damage, Core melt, Breach, Release) on each reactor's read-out, the goggles and Jade; alarm and fuel damage
can be fixed by taking the damaged fuel out. The Chicago Pile and Magnox can catch fire, the research reactor can crack
its fuel or lose its pool, a Magnox vessel can burst, and the RBMK can blow its lid off in a steam explosion that
throws radioactive fuel and graphite up to 40 blocks and starts a graphite fire. Any reactor that loses its cooling
after a shutdown can still melt from its decay heat. Fires are put out with sand, boron carbide or lead. Releases drift
downwind as a fallout plume and settle on the ground; molten cores flow like slow lava and set into the Elephant's
Foot. Fuel stored carelessly can go critical on its own (a warning sounds at 80% of the limit). Build a containment
building round a reactor to keep most of a release in, or entomb a wreck in a sarcophagus. Cleanup tools: Remote
Handling Tongs and a Shielded Container for debris, a Decontamination Station, a Topsoil Scraper, a Neutron Survey
Meter, potassium iodide and Prussian blue. Mobs and plants feel radiation too. Server settings for block damage,
explosion size, fallout and more. A new "Accidents" chapter in the Reactor Operator's Handbook (now 18 chapters, 94
pages), with the real accidents: Windscale 1957, Chernobyl 1986 and the "demon core". Worlds from 0.1.0-m7 carry
forward.

**Being built: 0.1.0-m9**
- **New screens.** Every Atomic Generation screen redone in one clear style: one big word across the top that says how
  the plant is doing (Running, Warning, Tripped ...), the few numbers that matter on bars that show safe and close to the
  limit at a glance, and alarms in plain words: what is happening and what to do. The goggles say the same thing.
- **Blueprints.** A cheap item for every multiblock: pick a build, see its size and the blocks it needs (what you have
  and what is missing), place a hologram to build against, or let it build itself from your inventory, a few blocks at
  a time. It never builds for free in survival and never breaks a block.

## DecayPlay's Guidebooks

**Out now: 1.0.0** (NeoForge 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.200 or newer, on both client and server; JEI is optional). The
first release of the guidebook library for DecayPlay's mods: one separate mod that the CurseForge app installs for you.
On its own it adds nothing; each mod brings its own book. **Guided pages:** what you need with a live count from your
inventory, numbered steps that tick themselves off, done when, if it doesn't work, and "What do I do next?" on the first
page. Search, bookmarks, back and forward; the book remembers your page. Pictures, and 3D views of blocks, items, mobs
and whole builds: one layer at a time, building up block by block, in each size, with the list of what it takes. Recipes
on the page, with "View in JEI" when JEI is installed. **Show in world:** a hologram of a build to build against. Right
blocks vanish, wrong blocks glow red, a list shows what is missing, and "Build complete" ticks the step. Hold G over an
item to open its page. Each mod's book is given on first join and has a cheap recipe. Resource packs can change any page.

**Planned: 1.1**
- Show in world also checks which way a block faces. Today the hologram checks which block you placed, not its
  direction, so a furnace or a controller put in facing the wrong way still counts as right. Where a build needs a
  block to face a certain way, the hologram will check that too.
