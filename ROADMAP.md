# DecayPlay's mods: roadmap

What's out, what's next and what's planned for each of my Minecraft mods. There are no dates: an update comes out
when it's built and tested.

Want something on this list? **[Post an idea](https://github.com/tyler919/mods/issues/new/choose)**, or give an
existing one a 👍. I look at the 👍 counts when I pick what goes in the next update.

| Word | Means |
|---|---|
| **Out now** | On CurseForge |
| **Being built** | Being made right now, for the next update |
| **Planned** | Decided, coming in a later update |
| **Ideas** | Being considered, not promised yet |

**Prefer clicking around?** The same roadmap is an [interactive board](https://github.com/users/tyler919/projects/2), with a tab for each mod.

**Want a ping when a mod updates?** Join [DecayPlay's Workshop on Discord](https://discord.gg/y6z5GpgBcr).

## Colossal Quarry

**Out now: 1.4.0** (NeoForge, 1.21.1; the Forge file stays on 1.2.0). Energy Port Output mode: take the rig's power
back out. Sneak + right-click an Energy Port and it gives the rig's stored FE back out: cables and batteries can pull
it, and it fills a battery placed right next to it. Input stays the default.

**Planned: 2.0, logistics**
- Storage Silo: a multiblock that holds huge amounts of a few item types
- Quarry Conveyor: links rigs to silos
- Pump upgrade + Fluid Port: keeps lava and water instead of deleting it
- Lava Generator and Heavy Lava Generator: burn lava (the Heavy one holds 120 buckets); the Heavy Generator is renamed the Heavy Coal Generator
- Extra drills upgrade: up to 4 drills share one dig, more the bigger the area (name not final)
- Pick-your-own list for the Ore Sieve upgrade
- Fill-back upgrade: leaves no hole
- XP Collector upgrade

**Planned: 3.0, the full rig**
- Bigger rigs: the Heavy Rig (7×7×7) and the Colossal Rig (9×9×9)
- Drill Heads that temper as they dig and grow into the next head
- Overdrive, kept cool with water
- Survey Scanner, and a Survey button that predicts the whole dig
- Dense Silo Casing (4× the space)
- Field Manual (an in-game guide book), advancements and a full set of sounds
- A charge slot on the generators, and optional CC:Tweaked support

**Ideas from players**
- Seal the edges of the hole so water and lava outside can't flow in ([#3](https://github.com/tyler919/mods/issues/3))
- A rig a creeper can't blow up ([#3](https://github.com/tyler919/mods/issues/3))

## Colossal Furnace

**Out now: 1.2.1** (NeoForge 1.21.1). Tiers 1 to 5 and Tier 20, the Heart slot, and an optional Liquid Experience
tank when XP Works is installed.

**Being built: 1.4.0**
- Lava from tanks: burn lava straight from a tank or fluid pipe from any mod, no buckets needed. The furnace only takes the lava it needs for what's cooking. At Tier 20, lava smelts 1,500 items per bucket

**Planned: 1.3.0**
- In-game Ponder guides: install Ponder and hold W over a Colossal Furnace block to see, step by step, how to build and use it
